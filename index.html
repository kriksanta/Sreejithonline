About me

A bit about who I am

I'm Sreejith, based in Attingal, Thiruvananthapuram. I spent most of my twenties in BPO service and sales teams, handling escalations that had already gone wrong once and coaching the people who would handle the next one. Since 2024 I've carried that into collections at BFIL.

The work is equal parts negotiation and record-keeping: understand why someone stopped paying, agree on something they can actually do, and document it the way RBI expects. I care about the recovery numbers — and about the borrower still taking my call next month.

I finished my BBA in 2025 alongside full-time work, and I'm steadily adding the certifications that go with the collections side of banking.
